Scene Building

Panels & Scenes

Your 3D scene exist only in the Panels of a Scene. If you make a new scene(i.e to indicate a cut) the 3D layout will disappear and it will return to a 2D scene. New Panels are made by right-clicking on the timeline and selecting New Panel or pressing [P] a new scene can be made by right-clicking and selecting New Panel

Moving Layers in 3D

To move layers in the 3D space, click the Layer Transform Tool on the left toolbar or press [cmd/crt+T]. This will give the 3D transform pop up on your selected layer where you can; move, scale and rotate your layer

Note

Once a layer is transformed, all drawings on said layer will lock to the plane of the layer

i.e If you are making a full 3D one layer must be one the x-axis of the box another for the y-axis and another for the z- axis

Animation Layer Keyframes

To do keyframe animation of layers:

  1. Set your play head to the desired start position
  2. click the stick man next to the layer that will be animated. (The icon will change and become yellow when activated)
  3. Scroll down on the timeline panel to see where the keyframe has been set
  4. Go to where the desired end point of the animation will be
  5. Use the Layer Transform Tool to move layer to desired position

Camera Animation Keyframes

Glitch

Sometimes the indicator to use the transform tools break and are not clickable. The Solution seems to be restarting the software for now

To do keyframe animation of camera:

  • Put the playhead to the desired position
  • Go to the Timeline Window and click on the [+] on the Camera section above the panel timeline to set the first keyframe for a panel
  • Set the playhead to the desired end and click the [+] again
  • Select the Camera tool on the left toolbar access camera movement tools
  • Move cameras to desired location

Note

Camera movements work across multiple Panels but NOT across a Scene separation.

Extra Tools

Tool Properties Window

Going into [Windows]>[Tool Properties] will bring up the Tool property windows. These have extra settings for all of your tools including [Camera] and [Layer Transform]

Access to more specific key frame control

Ability to snap layer to ground plane

Layers Window

[Windows]>[Layers] will bring up the Layers window which will give you more options with your layers (i.e Pinning layers to the camera)

[Layer Alignment] allows you to have a layer always face the camera or pin the layer to the camera
