Downloading add-ons

Add-ons can be downloaded by:

  1. If from the Blender Extension Store; by clicking the [Get Add-On] button and dragging directly onto your blender scene
  2. If the add-on was downloaded from outside of the Blender Extension store:
    • In Blender go to: [Edit]>[Preferences]
    • Click on the [Add-ons] tab
    • In the top right corner click on [v]
    • Click on [Install from disk]
    • Find the location and of the downloaded .zip file and select it

You can access the add-ons in your viewport panel by pressing [N] on the keyboard.
